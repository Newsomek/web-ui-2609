Quick Guide

How to explore PracticeSpend

PracticeSpend is an independent purchasing-intelligence demonstration. It analyzes invoice and purchasing evidence to surface cost changes, vendor differences, duplicates, reconciliation issues, contract discrepancies, purchasing patterns, and other items worth reviewing.

Start here

A five-minute tour

  1. Start on Overview. See total analyzed spend, potential annualized opportunities, major price changes, top vendors, and categories that may deserve attention.
  2. Open an Opportunity. Select a finding and choose See calculation. PracticeSpend shows the evidence and math behind the finding rather than asking you to trust a black-box score.
  3. Visit Invoices. Review the source purchasing records, line items, provenance, reconciliation state, and possible duplicate handling.
  4. Visit Products. Compare effective unit costs over time, filter by vendor, category, relationship, and invoice date, and inspect possible purchasing equivalents.
  5. Visit Vendors. Compare spend, fees, invoices, products, and findings by vendor. Select a vendor to inspect the supporting detail.
  6. Look at Reviews. This is where findings that require human judgment can be assigned, approved, rejected, reopened, or reviewed historically.
  7. Open Management. See how practice-controlled reference data and product classifications can be managed without rewriting the analysis.
  8. Open Data & Import. See how invoice data can be imported, manually entered, exported, locally saved, or reset.

Understanding the numbers

A few terms matter

Analyzed spend

Spend included in the current analysis after applicable filters and governance decisions such as duplicate suppression.

Potential annualized opportunity

An evidence-based estimate of a possible annualized financial opportunity. It is not guaranteed savings. Anomalies and review items are not silently treated as savings.

Effective Unit Cost

Merchandise after line discount + allocated shipping / handling / fees - allocated invoice or volume discount ÷ normalized units

This lets PracticeSpend compare the economic purchase cost of the same product even when pack sizes, shipping, fees, or discounts differ.

Credits and returns

Credits and returns remain separate financial adjustments. PracticeSpend does not silently relabel them as purchase discounts.

Red and green changes

Red + means effective unit cost increased. Green - means effective unit cost decreased. Plus/minus is always shown so color is never the only signal.

Human judgment

PracticeSpend does not pretend every answer is automatic

Some purchasing conclusions can be calculated directly from invoice evidence. Others require human judgment.

Example: Two differently named products may look like possible purchasing equivalents. PracticeSpend may identify the relationship for review, but it does not make a clinical equivalence determination.

Review decisions can record who decided, when they decided, their source or basis, notes, assignment history, and later changes. Prior decisions remain part of the history rather than simply disappearing.

Duplicates and reconciliation

Records are governed, not simply erased

A suspected duplicate can be confirmed as legitimate, suppressed, or permanently deleted. Suppression keeps the record and its history but excludes it from analysis. Permanent deletion requires explicit confirmation.

When an invoice does not reconcile, an authorized user can correct the financial fields from the source document. PracticeSpend records the before/after values, actor, time, source or basis, and resulting reconciliation state.

About this demonstration

What this version is - and is not

This demonstration is tailored to a fictional dermatology-practice purchasing scenario. Public physician names are used for the demo, but the purchasing data, operational staff, permissions, assignments, transactions, and decisions are fictional.

The current prototype runs in the browser and does not require a PracticeSpend backend. Imported data is processed locally in the browser and can optionally be persisted on that device.

Do not enter patient information. PracticeSpend is demonstrating business purchasing analysis, not patient or clinical record handling.

A production version would require additional work including production authentication, server-side security and audit controls, integrations, deployment architecture, testing, and operational hardening.
