# PracticeSpend — Competitive Positioning and Value

## Category

PracticeSpend begins as an **invoice-driven purchasing intelligence and spend-optimization system** for independent medical practices.

It is not primarily:

- accounts payable automation;
- bill payment;
- accounting;
- inventory management;
- procurement marketplace;
- credit card/expense management;
- EHR functionality.

## Core positioning

> **Don't replace your purchasing system. Audit it.**

PracticeSpend works with the vendors and systems a practice already uses to independently analyze what it actually paid, identify anomalies and opportunities, and show the evidence behind every finding.

## Why not just use a procurement platform?

PracticeSpend should require less commitment:

- no purchasing migration;
- no vendor lock-in;
- no marketplace requirement;
- no bank/card connection;
- no permission to spend money;
- no new approval workflow;
- historical analysis from existing exports/invoices.

## Why not just use ChatGPT?

General-purpose AI can inspect documents, but PracticeSpend should maintain capabilities that a one-off conversation does not naturally provide:

- persistent normalized product identity;
- vendor-SKU mapping;
- unit-of-measure normalization;
- longitudinal price history;
- effective-unit-cost calculations;
- contract/rebate rules;
- duplicate detection;
- confidence-controlled product equivalence;
- deterministic savings calculations;
- recurring findings;
- evidence traceability;
- eventual cross-practice benchmarking.

AI may assist with ingestion and normalization; PracticeSpend owns the structured financial memory and analytical model.

## Initial value proposition

PracticeSpend should help a practice answer:

- What did we spend?
- What changed?
- Where did prices creep upward?
- Where did we pay different prices for the same thing?
- Which fees and shipping costs accumulated?
- Where may negotiated pricing not have been honored?
- What deserves a conversation with a vendor?
- Which findings are verified, strong, or merely potential?

## High-value future features

### Vendor negotiation packet

Generate a defensible vendor review showing annual spend, price trends, alternative observations, fee patterns, and specific invoices supporting each discussion point.

### Quote comparison

Normalize multiple vendor quotes and compare effective effective unit cost rather than headline line-item price.

### Before-you-buy review

Analyze a proposed order before purchase and surface known historical/alternative pricing issues.

### Rebate / threshold intelligence

Model whether moving spend to cross a rebate/free-shipping/volume threshold is economically beneficial.

### Benchmarking

With appropriate future contracts, security, aggregation, and de-identification, PracticeSpend could tell a practice how its observed prices compare with aggregated peer ranges without exposing another customer's identifiable data.

This dataset may become one of the product's most defensible long-term assets.
